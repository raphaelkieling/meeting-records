# Meetily desktop recording

Checked on November 22, 2026 against Community `v0.5.0` and the published documentation for Pro `1.11.2`. [Community release](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/releases/tag/v0.5.0) · [Pro release index](https://meetily.ai/releases)

## Community

- Meetily opens separate microphone and system-audio streams. On macOS, the microphone uses CPAL, and system audio defaults to a Core Audio process tap with ScreenCaptureKit available. The live pipeline mixes the sources before voice activity detection, transcription, and recording. Source identity is lost at the mix, so the resulting transcript cannot derive mic/system speaker labels from those chunks. Meetily lists voice diarization as a Pro feature. [Edition comparison](https://meetily.ai/pricing) · [Stream startup](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/blob/v0.5.0/frontend/src-tauri/src/audio/stream.rs#L710-L774) · [macOS backend](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/blob/v0.5.0/frontend/src-tauri/src/audio/capture/backend_config.rs#L63-L83) · [Core Audio tap](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/blob/v0.5.0/frontend/src-tauri/src/audio/capture/core_audio.rs#L88-L95) · [Mixing pipeline](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/blob/v0.5.0/frontend/src-tauri/src/audio/pipeline.rs#L1130-L1176)
- With audio saving enabled, a completed meeting has one mixed mono AAC file, `audio.mp4`, beside `metadata.json` and `transcripts.json`. The app writes audio in checkpoints and joins them on stop. On macOS the default root is `~/Movies/meetily-recordings`, with a folder named from the meeting title and UTC timestamp. ## Pro

- Pro captures microphone and system audio. The Pro 1.7.3 release notes document three audio outputs per saved meeting consisting of a mix, a microphone file, and a system-audio file. A firsthand Windows 1.7.7 report lists `audio.mp4`, `audio_mic.mp4`, and `audio_system.mp4`. The current 1.11.2 release notes confirm separate capture sources, and the 1.9.2 notes document a configurable save location. The exact filenames and encoding in 1.11.2 remain unverified. [Capture in 1.11.2](https://meetily.ai/releases/pro/1.11.2) · [Per-channel files in 1.7.3](https://meetily.ai/releases/pro/1.7.3#host-vs-guest-speaker-identification) · [Windows 1.7.7 report](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/issues/578) · [Save location](https://meetily.ai/releases/pro/1.9.2#your-recordings-where-you-want-them)
- **Source labels** map microphone audio to **Host** and system audio to **Guest**. The labels describe the input, so several people on one input share a label when available. **Speaker diarization** is an optional Pro beta that groups distinct voices offline, including voices from a single input. It requires saved audio. For recorded meetings, final labels appear after Stop. Diarization also works for imports and retranscription, and users can name, merge, or correct speaker labels. [Source tagging](https://meetily.ai/releases/pro/1.7.3#host-vs-guest-speaker-identification) · [Current diarization guide](https://docs.meetily.ai/features/speaker-diarization)
